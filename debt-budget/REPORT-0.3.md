# Debt-and-budget accounting v0.3

This release connects synthetic fiscal settlements to the unchanged frozen operating controller. It is a research accounting model, not a national forecast, economic validation or external audit. The ten inherited fiscal scenarios remain available, including the nine cases with assumed authorized issuance and the independently computed allowance case. Releases 0.1 and 0.2 remain unchanged.

The independent allowance fixture starts at R110 (M109, T1, B0) with starting target110. Ten synthetic annual GDP observations are 2%; three first-release observations for each price series are100, with bases and target100 and inflation target0. The controller computes allowance 272961236526209/500000000000000, approximately0.545922473. Half is allocated to spending and half to principal retirement. Allocation is checked separately and cannot exceed the allowance. These figures are synthetic units, not dollars.

The new paired-ledger replay posts fiscal taxes, spending, borrowing, external debt redemption and coupons, Bill sales/maturities/coupons, conversion repayments and issuance through both ledgers. After each settlement it checks M, B, T, R, Bank and External money, external Treasury debt and conversion claims. A failed posting restores both ledgers, including compound auction registers. A separate full-quarter, zero-allowance same-day tax/spend case passes. Unsupported holder transactions reject explicitly.

The inherited allowance scenario's accounting balances still reconcile, but its replay has a policy FAIL: its single issuance payment fails the controller's monthly-tranche rule, and cash accumulated ahead of maturities violates Treasury cash limits. These failures are preserved rather than changing the example's timing to obtain a pass. Passing software tests confirm that these failures are detected; they do not certify the scenario's policy compliance. The borrowing-ban example still leaves 15 units of unpaid principal outstanding and overdue. Failed payment never cancels that liability.

The paired controller represents external Treasury debt only. Authority-held and intragovernmental securities remain explicit in the fiscal ledger but are outside that mirrored debt perimeter; settlements to those holders are blocked by the adapter. No Authority remittance or new C11 policy interpretation is supplied. Auction bids and contractual coupons are explicit synthetic inputs; no market demand, future rate, bank solvency or economic response is predicted. Cancellation remains a net-worth event, not an automatic annual cash saving.

Two separately labeled post-disclosure follow-ups exercise the approved interpretations. C05 uses a new external adapter that validates an expressly authorized actual-balance formula at the effective boundary and then calls the frozen controller's numeric reset interface; it does not claim the original controller natively stored a contingent formula. The example starts with R104/path100, settles a recovery of1 and resolves the reset to103. C06 explicitly starts at path600/R500 and applies annualized growth of4% to the path: permitted catch-up exceeds4% of R. A jump from starting path500 to600 is rejected. These are visible follow-up tests, not a new unseen challenge or a rewrite of the original underspecified C06 case.

Six raw official Treasury datasets dated September30,2026 were independently retrieved on November2,2026 and are packaged with their URLs, row counts and exact-byte hashes. These are historical observations, not synthetic inputs. Debt to the Penny's public and intragovernmental components sum to gross debt $40,171,825,101,340.31. MTS Table6 fiscal-year liability flows minus asset flows plus unapplied transactions equal the $1,992,473,224,352.42 budget deficit. This verifies selected identities, not a complete national opening balance sheet.

MSPD gross debt exceeds Debt to the Penny by $5,287.19. The components agree at nearest-million precision, but rounding has not been proved to explain the residual. DTP controls the historical stock. API interest exceeds MTS gross interest by $443,612,949.17, exactly the restoration-of-interest component. API public-issue interest matches MTS public issues; government-account interest less restoration matches MTS special issues. Treasury's FY2025 AFR Note16, page111 supports the December31,2025 OPM restoration event. The precise MTS classification/exclusion rationale remains unresolved; the expense is neither discarded nor counted as a saving.

MTS Table5 itself labels public issues on an accrual basis and special issues on a cash basis. Consequently its $1,051,753,969,311.11 net-interest line is not a pure cash coupon input. The $21,004,274,007.38 accrued-interest-payable increase is only one bridge item. Gross-to-net offsets, the complete cash bridge, Authority valuation, opening monetary assets, conversion base and contractual maturities remain unresolved. National scenario execution stays BLOCKED. Historical maturity rows have not been summed into a payoff schedule.
