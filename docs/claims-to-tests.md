# Claims and checks

| Narrow claim | Executable evidence | What it does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Entries balance across the modeled sectors | `audit()` after every accepted financial event | Completeness of real institutions and contracts |
| Fiscal transfers and bill principal settlement conserve R | Fiscal and bill tests in `test/accounting.test.mjs` | Market demand or legal authority |
| Unforecast issuance stays within one measured allowance | Forecast/duplicate-credit tests; remittance test | Price stability or optimal path |
| P moves to U at final legal discharge without changing R | Repayment, impairment and discharge tests | Recoveries or actual court outcomes |
| No new draw exceeds implemented cap headroom | Draw admission and generated paths | A rule for emergency-cap expiry overhang |
| A five-day daily-close cash cure is distinguishable from six days | Calendar/cure tests | Every possible intraday interpretation |
| The earlier nine synthetic year outputs are reproduced | Frozen oracle comparisons | Independent economic confirmation |
| Longer accounting sequences remain consistent | Eight seeded ten-year paths and one twenty-five-year path | Probability, stress forecasts, welfare or household prices |
| An expected invalid issuance can be minimized to a short trace | Counterexample deletion test and output | Global shortestness or successful exploitation |
| Unknown mechanisms remain visibly unknown | Undefined auction, expiry, rebase and recovery tests | A resolved production policy |

`npm test` counts software test cases, not economic scenarios or proof of the proposal. `npm run reproduce` generates the modeled transactions and explicit undefined example. No economic kill criteria concerning prices, credit contraction, foreign yields or payment access have been tested.
