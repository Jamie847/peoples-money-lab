# Design-paper source and refinancing record

Prepared 3 November 2026. This is a new technical status note, **not Design Paper 0.7.3** and not a historical amendment to 0.7.2.

Searches found Design Paper exports through the 31 October 2026 0.7.2 source. Book references to 0.7.3 do not establish its text, change list or date. Publication of a genuine 0.7.3 and a verified textual comparison remain blocked pending that source. Earlier versions have not been relabelled or edited.

The project's production log, dated November 1 and saved November 2 at 21:08:49 UTC, records a November 1 design decision by the author: refinancing allowed; the borrowing ban starts after a certified switch and statutory phase-in; Stabilization Bills outside the ban; a narrow time-limited emergency exception. It says the certifying test and responsible certifier remain open and that the Design Paper and model are to follow. This is a production record of the decision, not the original approval itself. The rest of that log concerns the manuscript and is not distributed.

On November 3 the author asked for a test of no new net borrowing with refinancing permitted, and for the November 1 decision to be recorded. That request is the basis for the new follow-up. It does not supply missing historical paper text or resolve the netting interval, advance refinancing rules, certification test, certifier or emergency ceiling. The new test therefore distinguishes same-day conditional refinancing from a quarter-net advance-funding sensitivity. Neither convention is silently adopted as final policy.
